# Fourfold: M9.1 Results — Background jobs and push

*Status: 2026-11-12, at the end of M9.1 ([milestones.md](milestones.md)). Records what was built for background jobs, schedules and SSE push, the choices the milestone made, and what's left for later. The second half of what was planned as M9 ([m9-results.md](m9-results.md)). No MS2 changes.*

## Summary

| Question | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Can work happen outside a request? | **Yes.** `job.x` in `code/jobs/` runs in a fresh VM, enqueued by code (`enqueue`) or on a schedule (`code/schedule.ms`), with its own budget, at most `config.jobs.workers` at once. |
| Is a job interrupted by a restart run again? | **Yes.** A job still marked running when the engine starts goes back in the queue (at-least-once). |
| Do a failed request's jobs and messages leak out? | **No.** `enqueue` and `publish` collect into the run; the engine commits them only if it succeeded (a job's too). |
| Do schedules keep time across DST? | **Yes**, in any IANA zone: a skipped time runs at the jump, a repeated one runs once. Tested in `America/Denver` for both 2026 changes. |
| Can pages get updates pushed to them? | **Yes**, over SSE on `/live/<name>`, with HTMX's SSE extension built into `/__fourfold/htmx.js`. |
| Can a visitor subscribe to a channel they shouldn't? | **No.** A channel is a path under `code/access.ms`'s rules, checked by the policy gate; one no rule covers isn't served. |
| Does the newsfeed sample work? | **Yes**: tested against a local stand-in, and by hand against Hacker News's API (stories pushed over SSE, the digest mailed). |

## What was built

- **`base`**: `TimeZone` (`time_zone.h`): IANA zones from TZif files (`$TZDIR` or `/usr/share/zoneinfo`), version 1 to 3, with the footer's POSIX rule for times past the last transition (`Mm.w.d`, `Jn` and `n` dates, any times); `offsetAt`, `toUtc` (which says whether a local time was skipped or repeated), `toLocal`; civil date helpers.
- **`compiledapp`**: `Schedule` and `parseSchedule` (`schedule.h`): the friendly forms and cron, `nextAfter(t, zone)`; `HandlerRef::Kind::Job`; `CompiledApp::jobs`, `schedule`, `accessRules` and `channelAccess(name)`; `isValidChannelName`, `kLivePrefix`.
- **`compiler`**: `code/jobs/**/*.ms` compiled with the scripts; `pass_jobs.cpp` (job names unique across files, `code/schedule.ms` evaluated as data and checked); `/live/` reserved in `code/pages/`; access rules kept for channels, and a rule at or below `/live` isn't "unused"; `"public"` as an access value; links to `/live/<name>` checked against the rules; `describe` lists jobs and their schedules.
- **`script`**: `Run::Kind::Job` and `ScriptHost::runJob` (the file's top level, then the job with frozen `args` as `_ff_req`; an error value fails it, anything else succeeds); job files get only a `job` map; the `enqueue` and `publish` intrinsics (`modules/jobs.cpp`); `JobRequest`, `PushMessage`; `HandlerOutcome::enqueued`, `published` (only on success) and `droppedJobs`; `RunContext::enqueue` and `publish`.
- **`state`**: `JobQueue` (`job_queue.h`) in `state.db`: jobs (queued, running, dead) with a partial unique index on the key while queued, attempts, errors and where they came from; the last 200 finished attempts; the schedule's state (last and next run, and the spec and zone it was computed for).
- **`config`**: `config.jobs` (`timeZone`, `workers`, `instructions`, `seconds`, `maxAttempts`), with server ceilings for `jobs.instructions`, `jobs.seconds` and `jobs.workers`.
- **`push`** (new component): `PushHub` (`push_hub.h`): channels and their streams on the transport's loop, `publish` from any thread, a comment every 15 s (which also finds closed streams), at most 1,000 subscribers; `pushEvent` formats one event, a `data:` line per line.
- **`jobs`** (new component): `JobRunner` (`job_runner.h`), on its own thread: requeues interrupted jobs at start; fires due schedule entries; claims due jobs up to the worker cap; runs each inside the schema lock; commits a success's effects, or records a failure with its retry time (or as dead); warns when a deploy removes a job that's still queued; the dev tools' status, run now, retry, discard and pause.
- **`pipeline`**: `/live/<name>` served by `Engine::serveChannel` (404 if no rule covers it, 405 for anything but GET, the policy gate for access, 503 when the hub is full); `Engine::jobServices` (a job's `RunContext`, from the config and app serving now) and `commitEffects` / `dropEffects`, used by requests and jobs alike; `EngineOptions::jobs`, `jobsAdded` and `push`; the SSE extension embedded in `/__fourfold/htmx.js`.
- **`observer`**: `jobsEnqueued(source, names, committed)`; dev mode's terminal notes jobs dropped by a failed request.
- **`devtools`**: `/__dev/jobs` and `/__dev/jobs.json` (the schedule with next and last runs in the app's zone, the queue, recent runs, dead jobs with retry and discard, run now, pause, and open channels with their subscriber counts); channels in the access report; "jobs" in the corner menu.
- **`template`**: an `.md` fragment rendered for HTML output also carries its Markdown form when that renders cleanly (so it can go into a mail template from a job or a request); an empty one always does.
- **`hosts/cli`**: `AppServer` opens the queue, makes the hub and the runner, wires the panel, wakes the runner on enqueues, recompiles and config reloads, and stops it before the script host. `ServerOptions::jobRetryDelay` (tests).
- **Sample:** `examples/newsfeed`: a job polls Hacker News every 5 minutes and publishes new stories to `/live/stories`; "Check now" enqueues it with a key; a daily digest at 07:00 `America/Denver` mails the owner from a Markdown template with a Markdown fragment.
- **Third party:** `third_party/htmx/sse.js`, htmx-ext-sse 2.2.2 (0BSD).

## Choices made here

- **Channels are paths under the access rules** (decided with the user): `/live/<name>`; a channel no rule covers is refused as an unmatched route would be.
- **IANA time zones** (decided with the user), from the system's files rather than a bundled database: one less thing to keep current, and the system's is updated with the OS. A zone that isn't there is a config error.
- **`"public"` as an access value.** Without it a public channel couldn't be declared (access values only required things). It requires nothing, so on a page it changes nothing, keeping "rules only tighten".
- **A job's enqueues commit only if it succeeds**, closing the overview's open question; chains work, and a failing job's follow-ups don't run once per attempt. **Publishes are held back too**, from requests and jobs, so subscribers never see something a failed run didn't keep.
- **Keys are unique while queued**, not while running: a digest enqueued during a running digest still runs (the data may have changed). A retrying job gives way to a newer one with its key.
- **Scheduled ticks go through the queue** with the key `schedule:<name>`. That gives no self-overlap (a tick is skipped while the last is queued or running), retries, the panel's view, and at-least-once delivery, with no second mechanism.
- **"every" is an interval since the last run**, starting when the schedule appears, rather than aligned to the clock; real time, so DST can't stretch it. Clock-aligned times use `hourly`, `daily` or cron.
- **Missed runs run once**: a schedule's next time is stored; at startup, an overdue one fires once and the next is computed from now.
- **A schedule's state is recomputed when its text or the zone changes** (the stored spec is `"<text> @ <zone>"`).
- **The pause toggle pauses the schedule only**, as the overview's dev-mode section says; queued jobs and "run now" still work.
- **Jobs share the script thread and the schema lock with requests**: the worker cap (2) bounds how much of the thread they take, and migrations wait for running jobs as for requests.
- **Job retries**: 15 s, doubling, at most an hour; `maxAttempts` 5.
- **`publish` refuses HTML holding the publisher's CSRF or timing token**: a form rendered for one visitor would otherwise hand their token to every subscriber. It also refuses `<script>` (no nonce on the pages it reaches) and escapes strings.
- **HTMX's SSE extension is served inside `/__fourfold/htmx.js`**, so `hx-ext="sse"` works on any page that loads htmx.
- **`.md` fragments carry their Markdown form on HTML output when they can**, found when the sample's digest (a job putting an `.md` fragment into a mail template) failed: jobs and HTML requests rendered fragments for HTML only.

## Tests

- `tests/base/time_zone_test.cpp` (new): civil dates; UTC and names that can't be zones; `America/Denver`'s offsets across both 2026 changes, and far past and future (the footer rule); skipped, repeated and ordinary local times; the rule alone matching the file all year; Sydney's southern-hemisphere rule; quoted names; bad rules and files.
- `tests/compiledapp/schedule_test.cpp` (new): every form and its next time; cron lists, ranges, steps, names, weekday 7, day-or-weekday, Feb 29; each mistake's message; **DST in Denver**: `daily 02:30` runs at the jump on March 8, then 02:30 MDT; `daily 01:30` runs once on November 1; `hourly :30` runs once in the repeated hour, including when started inside it; `every 1h` is real time.
- `tests/state/job_queue_test.cpp` (new): claim order and delays; keys (unique while queued, a retry giving way); retries, dead, retry and discard; jobs left running requeued, with a keyed one giving way; the schedule's state; trimmed history.
- `tests/compiler/jobs_test.cpp` (new): jobs found across files and listed; duplicate names, non-functions, two parameters, a `job` in a page file, schedule mistakes, `code/pages/live/`; channels' access from the rules (including `"public"` and `/`), and no "unused rule" warnings for them.
- `tests/hosts/cli/jobs_test.cpp` (new), end to end:
  - a request's job runs; a job's follow-up runs; **a failed request's enqueue is dropped** (and noted), and its publish never sent; bad args, an unknown job and a bad channel are errors;
  - **a key enqueues once** while pending; a delay holds a job;
  - a failing job is retried (at the test's short delay) and dead after `maxAttempts`, with what it enqueued and published dropped each time; retry, discard and run now from the panel, and the panel refusing another site's post;
  - **a job killed mid-run** (the server stopped while it waits) is left running in the queue, and runs again when the server starts;
  - **missed runs run once**: a schedule three hours overdue runs once at start, and its next time is five minutes on; the panel shows it, and pausing;
  - **push**: the SSE extension in htmx.js; a request publishes to a public channel's subscriber; **a members-only channel refuses a visitor who isn't signed in** (401) and lets a member in; an admin channel refuses a member (403); events by name go only to their channel; an uncovered channel is 404 and a POST 405; a failed request publishes nothing; a form with the visitor's token can't be published; the panel and access report list channels;
  - **a feed-polling job publishes new items** to an open subscriber, each once, against a stand-in feed;
  - **the newsfeed sample** compiles and serves (with the network shut off), shows its schedule in `America/Denver`, and its digest mails the owner.
- `tests/compiler/routes_test.cpp`: `"public"` is now valid alone, still not as a role in a list.

All 22 suites pass with the `dev` and `asan` presets, with `FOURFOLD_TEST_PG_URL` set.
